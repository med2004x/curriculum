# FSEGS LIG — Unnamed “une matière au choix” slots

**Semester:** S1 and S2  
**Attached engineering lesson:** 001 — Programs, Machines, and the Terminal

The supplied LIG checklist records an unnamed elective slot for each semester. The current official maquette and selected ECUE titles were not included in this repository. Verify the slot with your institution and instructor; do not invent a subject.
## What is already covered

The semester packs cover the subjects named in the supplied checklist. This file captures the exact choice-slot course once your section confirms it; compare all other packs against the official maquette as well.

## Exercises

1. Record the exact official title from the syllabus or teacher announcement.
2. Record the ECUE code, semester, coefficient, and teaching hours if provided.
3. Copy the chapter headings exactly as given in the official handout.
4. For every chapter, write five definitions that you must recall without notes.
5. For every chapter, create one worked example and annotate the reasoning.
6. Create at least ten blank exercises distributed across the published chapters.
7. Create two exercises that combine concepts from different chapters.
8. Create one unseen-data problem where the numbers/examples are changed from classwork.
9. Create one 30-minute timed mini-test with no references.
10. Create one cumulative mock covering every published chapter.

## How to study the slot

Treat this ECUE exactly like the named first-year subjects. Lecture notes are for understanding. The blank exercises are for retrieval. The changed-data problem checks transfer. The timed test checks exam execution.

For each chapter, maintain four pieces of evidence: a one-page concept sheet, solved class examples, blank exercises attempted without copying, and one timed question. Mark every mistake by type: missing knowledge, wrong method, arithmetic/calculation error, interpretation error, or time-management error.

Do not use the engineering lesson as a substitute for the official course content. Some choice subjects may have no programming component at all. The academic syllabus determines what is examinable.

## Mastery gate

The slot is mastered only when you can reproduce the chapter structure from memory, define the required vocabulary, solve standard exercises from blank, solve a changed-data problem, and complete the cumulative mock under the same time constraints used by the lecturer.

Until the official title and syllabus are captured, the correct action is to leave this slot marked “pending” rather than fabricate coverage. Once the title is known, create `CHOICE-S1-<short-name>.md` and/or `CHOICE-S2-<short-name>.md` in the matching semester folder and move the exercises into that subject file.
