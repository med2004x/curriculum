## Study contract

For each subject, follow the pack's scope, worked examples, exercises, problem-solving lab, and mastery gate. Keep written derivations and evidence in a personal portfolio. The semester master pack is a timed, closed-book rehearsal after the subject packs are complete.

The local packs provide a structured study route, not an official transcript or guarantee of syllabus coverage. Compare each pack with the current official course outline and instructor materials before exams; the elective subject remains unnamed until the institution confirms it.
