# Choose your starting route

## If you are studying the LIG first year

1. Choose [S1](LIG/S1/README.md) or [S2](LIG/S2/README.md).
2. Use the subject pack for each course and its evidence list.
3. Open the linked engineering lesson when the pack asks for it.
4. Save first attempts and corrections; take the semester mastery check under timed conditions only after studying the packs.
5. Verify the local pack against the current official syllabus.

## If you want the full systems/backend engineering path

Start at [Lesson 001](lessons/001-programs-machines-and-the-terminal/README.md) and follow the numbered engineering lessons in order. Each lesson links its lecture, practical work, evidence file, and code workspace.

The LIG semester organization and the full engineering sequence are related routes with overlapping support lessons; they are not two halves of a single 100-lesson semester count.
