# LIG academic tracks

Academic subject packs are separated by semester under [`LIG/`](LIG/README.md).

- [LIG Semester 1](LIG/S1/README.md)
- [LIG Semester 2](LIG/S2/README.md)
- [Shared elective slots](LIG/Shared/CHOICE-SLOTS.md)

Each semester index maps subject packs to supporting DART engineering lessons and practice files. The packs present actual subject material for algorithms, logic, analysis, algebra, statistics/probability, accounting, automata, OS, databases, multimedia, finance, management, and communication. This is a local study resource; compare it with the current official syllabus before relying on it as an exam checklist.
